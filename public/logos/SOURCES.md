# Organization logo sources

These marks identify the organizations associated with Savya’s experience and education. Their colors and proportions are preserved.

- Bruin Software Engineers: https://bseatucla.com/bse-logo-padded.png (official organization website)
- Brown University: https://ouc.brown.edu/themes/custom/brown/static/images/logo.svg (official university communications website). The `logo` symbol was placed into a standalone SVG, preserving its viewBox and artwork.
- UCLA: https://www.ucla.edu/img/logo_UCLA_white.svg (official university website)
- Fairleigh Dickinson University: https://www.fdu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/2-FDUmark2_2C-Update23.jpg (official university graphic standards downloads)

The Builder Bears entry uses a typographic team number, 10366, because a team-specific official logo could not be verified. It is not presented as an official logo.
